What this page is

  • A gateway, not a game hub. Every link leads to a real learning platform.
  • Grade-matched. Grades 4–12 each see age-appropriate picks.
  • Certificate-friendly. Many resources offer a badge or certificate on completion — good for portfolios.
  • No shooters, no Roblox-style free-for-alls. Curated by staff, not an algorithm.

Choose your grade

Tap your grade number. The list below updates to match your age group.

How these are chosen: every site listed here is reviewed by staff for two things — does it teach a real, transferable skill, and is it appropriate for the age group. We deliberately leave out first-person shooters, open social sandboxes like Roblox, and anything ad-heavy or with unmoderated chat. If you think something's missing or something needs re-checking, tell your ICT teacher.
